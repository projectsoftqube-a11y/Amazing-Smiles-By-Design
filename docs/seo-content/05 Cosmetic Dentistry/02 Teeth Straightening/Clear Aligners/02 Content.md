## How Clear Aligners Work

Each aligner in your series is slightly different, so your teeth shift gradually into position.

- Wear each aligner for about one to two weeks before switching to the next.
- Wear your aligners 20 to 22 hours a day.
- Remove them only to eat, to drink anything other than water, and to brush and floss.

## Clear Aligners vs. Braces

| | Invisalign clear aligners | Traditional metal braces |
|---|---|---|
| Appearance | Thin and transparent; hard to notice | Visible brackets and wires |
| Eating | Remove aligners, so no food restrictions | Some foods must be avoided |
| Comfort | Smooth plastic; no brackets or wires to irritate | Brackets and wires can irritate |
| Brushing and flossing | Remove aligners and clean normally | Special cleaning tools often needed |
| What it requires | Wearing aligners 20 to 22 hours a day; new trays can feel snug for a few days | Regular adjustment visits |

Straighter teeth are also easier to clean, which can help reduce the risk of cavities and gum disease.

## The Invisalign Treatment Process

1. **Consultation:** the dentist examines your teeth, discusses your goals and decides whether Invisalign is right for you.
2. **Digital scans and planning:** scans create a precise 3D model of your mouth and a plan for how your teeth will move.
3. **Fitting:** your custom aligners are checked for fit. Small tooth-colored attachments may be placed on some teeth to help guide movement.
4. **Wearing your aligners:** you switch to a new aligner about every two weeks, depending on your plan. Many patients notice changes within the first few months.
5. **Check-ups:** visits about every six weeks let the dentist monitor progress and give you your next aligners.

---

## How Long Does Invisalign Take?

For many patients, treatment takes about 9 to 15 months and involves 18 to 30 aligners. Some cases need more or less time. Wearing your aligners as directed helps keep treatment on schedule.

## After Invisalign

Retainers are typically recommended to keep teeth from shifting back. Many patients choose clear retainers. Some also brighten their new smile with [professional teeth whitening](/cosmetic-dentistry/teeth-whitening/).

## Why Grinding Matters

Many people grind or clench their teeth during sleep without realizing it. Grinding forces can be stronger than normal chewing forces, and over time they can wear down enamel, chip or crack teeth and strain the jaw muscles and joints.

### Common causes

- Stress or anxiety
- Sleep disorders
- Bite misalignment
- Certain medications
- Jaw tension or muscle strain

---

## Signs You May Need a Night Guard

- Frequent or morning headaches
- Jaw soreness or tightness
- Tooth sensitivity
- Worn or flattened teeth
- Chipped or cracked teeth
- Clicking or popping in the jaw
- Disrupted sleep

If you notice these signs, a dental evaluation can show whether you're grinding and whether a night guard could help.

---

## Benefits of a Night Guard

- **Protects enamel** from wear and the sensitivity that follows.
- **Reduces jaw strain** by spreading pressure more evenly.
- **Helps prevent cracks and fractures** caused by grinding forces.
- **Can help you sleep more comfortably** by easing jaw tension.

A night guard also helps protect cosmetic and restorative work such as [porcelain veneers](/cosmetic-dentistry/porcelain-veneers/) and [crowns](/restorative-dentistry/dental-crowns/).

## How We Make Your Night Guard

1. **Evaluation:** the dentist checks your teeth and jaw for signs of grinding or clenching.
2. **Digital scan or impression:** captures the exact shape of your teeth and bite.
3. **Fabrication:** your guard is made from durable materials designed for nightly grinding forces.
4. **Fitting:** you return so the dentist can make sure it fits comfortably and securely.

Most patients adapt to wearing a custom night guard very quickly.

---

## Caring for Your Night Guard

- Rinse it after each use
- Brush it gently with a toothbrush and mild soap
- Let it air dry before storing
- Keep it in its protective case
- Bring it to dental visits so it can be checked for wear

With proper care, a custom night guard can last several years.

## Night Guard FAQs

### Do I need a night guard?
Frequent or morning headaches, jaw soreness, tooth sensitivity, worn or flattened teeth, chipped teeth or clicking in the jaw can be signs of grinding or clenching. These symptoms can have other causes, so a dental evaluation can show whether a night guard would help.

### Is a custom night guard better than a store-bought one?
A custom night guard is molded precisely to your teeth, is more comfortable for overnight wear and is made from stronger, longer-lasting materials than generic over-the-counter guards.

### How long does a night guard last?
With proper care, a custom night guard can last several years. Bring it to your dental visits so it can be checked for wear.

### How do I clean my night guard?
Rinse it after each use, brush it gently with a toothbrush and mild soap, let it air dry and keep it in its protective case.

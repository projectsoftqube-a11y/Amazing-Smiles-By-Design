# Dental Bonding in Bensalem

Dental bonding is a minimally invasive cosmetic procedure that applies tooth-colored composite resin to a tooth, then shapes, hardens and polishes it to repair chips, close small gaps and improve the tooth's shape. ## What Dental Bonding Can Fix

- Chipped or cracked teeth
- Small gaps between teeth
- Minor tooth discoloration
- Uneven or misshapen teeth
- Areas of worn enamel
- Slightly short or irregular teeth

The composite resin is similar to the material used for [tooth-colored fillings](/restorative-dentistry/dental-fillings/) and is color-matched to your enamel.

---

## Benefits of Bonding

- **Natural appearance:** the resin is matched to your tooth color.
- **Conservative:** typically little to no natural tooth structure is removed.
- **Quick results:** many bonding treatments are completed in one appointment.
- **Affordable:** bonding is often more cost-effective than veneers or crowns.

---

## The Bonding Process

1. **Preparation:** the tooth surface is gently roughened so the resin adheres securely.
2. **Application:** tooth-colored resin is applied and sculpted to the right size, shape and contour.
3. **Hardening:** a curing light hardens the resin and bonds it to the tooth.
4. **Polishing:** the bonding is adjusted and polished to blend with your other teeth.

## Bonding vs. Veneers

| | Dental bonding | Porcelain veneers |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Composite resin applied directly to the tooth | Custom porcelain shells made in a lab |
| Visits | Often one | Several (planning, preparation, placement) |
| Tooth preparation | Little to none | A very small amount of enamel may be removed |
| Lifespan | Several years | Often well over a decade with proper care |
| Stain resistance | Lower; composite can stain over time | High; porcelain resists stains |
| Cost | Often more cost-effective | A larger investment |

## How Long Does Dental Bonding Last?

Dental bonding can last several years with proper care. Good oral hygiene and avoiding habits such as chewing on hard objects help extend its life, and regular checkups let the dentist monitor it.

## Dental Bonding FAQs

### How long does dental bonding last?
Dental bonding can last several years with proper care, including good oral hygiene, avoiding chewing on hard objects and regular dental checkups.

### Is dental bonding done in one visit?
Many dental bonding treatments can be completed in a single appointment.

### Does dental bonding damage your teeth?
Bonding is a conservative treatment that typically requires little to no removal of natural tooth structure. The tooth surface is gently roughened so the resin can adhere.

### Is bonding cheaper than veneers?
Bonding is often a more cost-effective way to improve the appearance of teeth than veneers or crowns.

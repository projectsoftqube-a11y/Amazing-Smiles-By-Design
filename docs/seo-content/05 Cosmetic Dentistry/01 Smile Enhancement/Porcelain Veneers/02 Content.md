## What Veneers Can Fix

Veneers can correct several cosmetic concerns at once, which is why many patients choose them as part of a complete smile makeover. They may improve:

- Discoloration or deep staining
- Chipped or cracked teeth
- Gaps between teeth
- Worn enamel
- Misshapen or uneven teeth
- Short teeth or irregular tooth size
- Minor alignment issues

---

## Will Veneers Look Natural?

Porcelain reflects light much like natural enamel, and each veneer is matched to the color of your surrounding teeth. Most people can't tell the difference between a porcelain veneer and a natural tooth. Porcelain is also stain resistant to coffee, tea and many foods.

### Functional benefits
Veneers cover and reinforce the front surface of chipped or worn teeth, help restore their shape for biting and chewing, and in some situations shield weakened enamel.

---

## Are You a Candidate?

You may benefit from veneers if you have discolored teeth that don't respond well to whitening, small gaps, uneven tooth shapes, worn or chipped enamel, or minor cosmetic misalignment. At your consultation, the dentist evaluates your oral health and goals to decide whether veneers are your best option.

---

## Porcelain Veneers vs. Dental Bonding

Porcelain veneers are made in a dental laboratory and are stain resistant and long-lasting. [Dental bonding](/cosmetic-dentistry/dental-bonding/) uses tooth-colored composite resin applied directly to the tooth, often in a single visit, and is often a more cost-effective option for smaller fixes. The dentist will help you compare them.

## The Veneer Process

### 1. Smile design and planning
The dentist evaluates your teeth, bite and facial structure to choose the ideal size, shape and shade. Digital imaging and smile design technology can help you visualize how your final smile may appear before treatment begins.

### 2. Preparing the teeth
A very small amount of enamel may be gently removed from the front of the teeth so the veneers don't look bulky. Impressions or digital scans are taken, and your veneers are made in a dental laboratory. Temporary veneers may sometimes be placed so you can preview your new smile.

### 3. Placement
The dentist checks the color, shape and fit, then bonds your veneers with dental adhesives.

## How Long Do Porcelain Veneers Last?

With proper care, porcelain veneers can last well over a decade. Longevity depends on your oral hygiene, habits and routine dental care.

---

## Caring for Your Veneers

- Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste
- Floss every day
- Visit the dentist regularly for cleanings and exams
- Don't chew on hard objects such as pens or ice
- Wear a [night guard](/cosmetic-dentistry/night-guards/) if you grind your teeth
- Use a sports mouthguard during athletic activities

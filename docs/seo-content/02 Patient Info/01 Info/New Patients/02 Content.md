## What Happens at Your First Visit

At your first visit, we will take the time needed to:

1. **Diagnose your immediate dental concerns.**
2. **Review your past medical and dental history.**
3. **Complete a thorough, comprehensive dental evaluation** with your cooperation.
4. **Create a treatment plan** that provides for your optimal dental health.

A new patient exam and cleaning visit includes an exam, X-rays and a cleaning if applicable.

Why do we take this time? Because the foundation of a long-term relationship is established at your first visit to our practice.

## New Patient FAQs

### Is Amazing Smiles By Design accepting new patients?
Yes. Amazing Smiles By Design in Bensalem, PA is welcoming new patients. Call or text (215) 639-5331, or request an appointment online.

### What happens at a new patient dental visit?
We diagnose your immediate dental concerns, review your past medical and dental history, complete a thorough, comprehensive dental evaluation and create a treatment plan for your optimal dental health.

### Does the first visit include a cleaning?
A new patient exam and cleaning visit includes an exam, X-rays and a cleaning if applicable.

### Do you have specials for new patients?
Yes. New patients can get an emergency exam, including the necessary X-rays, for a one-time fee of $59. Patients without insurance can also join one of our membership plans.

### Which days can I book?
The office is open Monday 8 am to 6 pm, Tuesday 8 am to 5 pm, Wednesday 8 am to 6 pm and Thursday 8 am to 2 pm, with morning and afternoon appointments.

## What Is Arestin?

Arestin contains minocycline, an antibiotic that fights the bacteria behind gum inflammation and infection. It comes as tiny microspheres. These are placed into periodontal pockets: the spaces that form between the teeth and gums when gum disease develops. Once placed, the medicine releases the antibiotic slowly. This helps reduce bacteria so the gums can heal.

---

## How Arestin Works With a Deep Cleaning

Arestin is typically used after [scaling and root planing](/general-dentistry/scaling-and-root-planing/), the deep cleaning that removes plaque, tartar and bacteria from beneath the gum line. Where deeper pockets remain, Arestin may be placed to help clear the bacteria left behind. The cleaning removes buildup, and the antibiotic works on the bacteria that remain.

Because the antibiotic is applied directly to the affected area, it targets harmful bacteria while minimizing exposure to the rest of the body.

### Benefits of Arestin

- **Targeted infection control:** the antibiotic is concentrated in the infected gum pocket.
- **Keeps working after your visit:** the slow-release formula continues to fight bacteria after you leave.
- **Supports gum healing:** reducing bacteria helps the gums reattach more securely to the teeth.
- **Non-surgical:** in many cases, it can help improve gum health without more invasive surgery.

---

## What to Expect

Placing Arestin is quick and comfortable. It usually happens after scaling and root planing. The dentist or hygienist places the medicine into the gum pocket with a small applicator. It is usually painless and doesn't need extra anesthesia, and the medicine starts working right away.

### Aftercare

You may be advised to:

- Avoid flossing the treated area for several days
- Eat softer foods for a while, if recommended
- Keep up your regular brushing and oral hygiene
- Attend follow-up visits so we can monitor healing

Long-term, regular dental visits, good oral hygiene and [periodontal maintenance](/general-dentistry/periodontal-maintenance/) help keep gum disease under control.

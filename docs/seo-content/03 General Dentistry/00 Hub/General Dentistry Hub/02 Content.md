## Gum Health: Deep Cleaning, Periodontal Maintenance and Arestin

Gum disease often develops quietly below the gum line. We treat and manage it with:

- [Scaling and root planing (deep cleaning)](/general-dentistry/scaling-and-root-planing/): non-surgical treatment that removes plaque, tartar and bacteria below the gum line.
- [Periodontal maintenance](/general-dentistry/periodontal-maintenance/): ongoing gum care, often every three to four months, after gum disease has been treated.
- [Arestin](/general-dentistry/arestin/): an antibiotic placed directly into infected gum pockets after a deep cleaning.

## General Dentistry FAQs

### What is general dentistry?
General dentistry is the everyday dental care that keeps teeth and gums healthy, including checkups, cleanings, X-rays, fillings, gum treatment, extractions and emergency care. A general dentist is usually your first stop for any dental concern.

### Is Amazing Smiles By Design a family dentist in Bensalem?
Yes. Amazing Smiles By Design in Bensalem, PA provides general and family dentistry for adults and children, including children's dentistry, from its office at 3101 Bristol Road, Suite 1.

### Is the practice accepting new patients?
Yes. New patients are welcome. Call or text (215) 639-5331, or request an appointment online.

### How often should my family see the dentist?
Preventive dental visits typically occur every six months. Patients who have been treated for gum disease often need periodontal maintenance visits every three to four months.

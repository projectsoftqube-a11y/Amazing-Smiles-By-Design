## What Is a Dental Emergency?

A dental emergency is any condition that threatens your teeth, gums or surrounding tissues, or causes significant discomfort. You may need urgent care if you have:

- Severe or persistent tooth pain
- Facial, gum or jaw swelling
- Bleeding that does not stop
- Signs of infection or an abscess
- Trauma to the teeth or mouth

When you text or call, our team will help determine how quickly you should be seen and arrange a same-day dental appointment whenever possible.

## Toothache and Tooth Infections

A severe toothache can be a sign of deep decay or an infection, and hidden infections can get worse quickly if ignored. An abscess may cause swelling, pain and a bad taste. Don't wait for it to pass: call or text us. Treatment may include a [root canal](/restorative-dentistry/non-surgical-root-canal/) to save the tooth or, if it can't be saved, a [tooth extraction](/general-dentistry/tooth-extraction/).

## Broken, Cracked or Knocked-Out Teeth

Some injuries, such as trauma from a fall or impact, are clearly urgent. Because the mouth has many blood vessels, even small injuries can bleed heavily. That can look alarming but doesn't always need emergency treatment. If you're unsure, text or call us. You don't need to diagnose the problem yourself; our team will listen and tell you whether immediate or same-day care is recommended.

## Getting Here From the Far Northeast

The office is about 4.5 miles from Parkwood, typically an 11-minute drive via Richlieu Road. Mechanicsville Road (about 11 minutes) and Knights Road (about 12 minutes) are alternatives. Times are Google Maps estimates and change with traffic.

Parkwood (ZIP 19154) is bordered by the Poquessing Creek, Knights Road and Woodhaven Road, on land that was once Byberry Township before Philadelphia annexed it in 1854. Across the Poquessing Creek is Bensalem.

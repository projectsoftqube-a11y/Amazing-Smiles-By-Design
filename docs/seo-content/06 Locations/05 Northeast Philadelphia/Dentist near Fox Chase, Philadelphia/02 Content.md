## Dental Implants

Dental implants replace the root of a missing tooth with a titanium or zirconia post, topped with a custom crown, bridge or full-arch restoration.

- Single-tooth implants, full-arch options and [implant-supported dentures](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/)
- Planned with detailed imaging, including CBCT scans, to check bone, nerves and sinuses
- The implant bonds with the bone over several months before the final crown is placed

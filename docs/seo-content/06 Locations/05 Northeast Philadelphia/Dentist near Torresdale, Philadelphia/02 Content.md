## What a Thorough Checkup Includes

Preventive dental visits typically occur every six months. Each visit can include:

- A review of your medical and dental history
- An exam of your teeth, existing fillings and crowns, bite and jaw
- A gum health check, measuring the pockets around each tooth
- An [oral cancer screening](/general-dentistry/oral-cancer-screening/) of your lips, tongue, cheeks, throat and neck
- A professional cleaning and digital X-rays when needed

## If Your Gums Need More Care

Bleeding, swollen or receding gums can be signs of gum disease. Treatment may include a [deep cleaning (scaling and root planing)](/general-dentistry/scaling-and-root-planing/), [Arestin](/general-dentistry/arestin/) for deeper pockets and [periodontal maintenance](/general-dentistry/periodontal-maintenance/) visits, often every three to four months.

## Tooth Pain: What Happens Next

1. **Call or text first.** Describe what's going on at (215) 639-5331. Our team will tell you how soon you should be seen and offers same-day appointments whenever possible.
2. **Exam and X-rays.** The dentist finds the cause. New patients can use the $59 Emergency Visit Special, which covers the necessary exam and X-rays.
3. **Treatment options.** Depending on the tooth, that may be a [filling](/restorative-dentistry/dental-fillings/), a [root canal](/restorative-dentistry/non-surgical-root-canal/) to save it, a [crown](/restorative-dentistry/dental-crowns/) to protect it, or an [extraction](/general-dentistry/tooth-extraction/) if it can't be saved.
4. **Replacing a lost tooth.** [Implants](/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants/), [bridges](/restorative-dentistry/dental-bridges/) or [dentures](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/).

*Severe facial swelling, trouble breathing or swallowing, or bleeding that won't stop? Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.*

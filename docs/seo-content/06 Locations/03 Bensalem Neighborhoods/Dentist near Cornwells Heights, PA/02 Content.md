## Replacing Missing Teeth

If you're missing one tooth or many, there are several options to compare:

| Option | How it's held in place | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| [Dental implant](/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants/) | A titanium or zirconia post in the jawbone | Helps preserve jawbone; planned with detailed imaging, including CBCT scans |
| [Bridge](/restorative-dentistry/dental-bridges/) | Anchored to the neighboring teeth | Usually two to three visits |
| [Partial or full denture](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/) | Rests on the gums (partials use clasps on nearby teeth) | Immediate dentures can be placed right after extractions |
| Implant-supported denture | Attaches to implants | Significantly less movement when you eat and speak |

The right option depends on an exam of your teeth, gums and jawbone. Dr. Dudhat has advanced training in implants. Financing is available through CareCredit and Cherry. [Financing options](/patient-information/financing-options/)

## Keeping Dentures and Implants Healthy

Denture wearers should have an exam once a year, and relines are typically recommended every one to two years to keep the fit comfortable. Like natural teeth, implants need daily brushing and flossing and regular checkups.

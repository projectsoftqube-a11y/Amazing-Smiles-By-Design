## Fixing a Problem Tooth

Most dental problems start small. Finding them early usually means simpler treatment:

- **Cavity:** a [tooth-colored filling](/restorative-dentistry/dental-fillings/).
- **Larger damage:** an [inlay or onlay](/restorative-dentistry/inlays-onlays/), or a [crown](/restorative-dentistry/dental-crowns/) for a cracked or weak tooth (usually two visits).
- **Infection:** a [non-surgical root canal](/restorative-dentistry/non-surgical-root-canal/) to remove the infection and save the tooth.
- **A tooth that can't be saved:** an [extraction](/general-dentistry/tooth-extraction/), then an [implant](/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants/), [bridge](/restorative-dentistry/dental-bridges/) or [denture](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/).

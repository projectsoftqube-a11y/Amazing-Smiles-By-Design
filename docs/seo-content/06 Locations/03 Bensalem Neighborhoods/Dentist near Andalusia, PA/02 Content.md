## Healthy Gums for Life

Gum disease often develops quietly, so every exam includes a gum health check:

- [Scaling and root planing (deep cleaning)](/general-dentistry/scaling-and-root-planing/) removes bacteria below the gum line.
- [Arestin](/general-dentistry/arestin/), a localized antibiotic, may be placed in deeper pockets.
- [Periodontal maintenance](/general-dentistry/periodontal-maintenance/) visits, often every three to four months, help keep gum disease under control.

No insurance? The Perio Maintenance Plan is $450 a year for 4 periodontal maintenance visits, 2 exams and screenings, routine X-rays and an emergency exam. [Membership plans](/specials/)

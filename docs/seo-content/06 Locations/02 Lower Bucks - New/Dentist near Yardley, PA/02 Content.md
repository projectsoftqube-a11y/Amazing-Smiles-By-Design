## Dentures, Explained

| Type | Best for | Key point |
|---|---|---|
| Full dentures | Replacing a whole arch | Upper dentures cover the palate; lower dentures leave room for the tongue |
| Partial dentures | Replacing some teeth | Held with clasps on nearby teeth; keeps teeth from shifting |
| Immediate dentures | When teeth must be removed | Placed right after extractions, so you don't go without teeth while you heal |
| Implant-supported dentures | More stability | Ball, bar or screw-retained designs that can significantly reduce movement |

Relines are typically recommended every one to two years, and an annual denture exam checks your gums, bone, fit and wear. [Dentures](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/)

## Yardley Patient FAQs

### How far is Amazing Smiles By Design from Yardley, PA?
About 12.2 miles, typically an 18-minute drive via I-295 West, according to Google Maps.

### What are immediate dentures?
Immediate dentures are made in advance from impressions taken before your extractions and placed right after your teeth are removed, so you don't go without teeth while you heal. Adjustments or relines are often needed as the gums heal.

### How often do dentures need to be relined?
Relines are typically recommended every one to two years to keep dentures fitting well.

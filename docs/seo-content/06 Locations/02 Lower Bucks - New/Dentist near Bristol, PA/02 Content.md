## About Bristol

Bristol is a borough on the Delaware River, across from Burlington, New Jersey. Settled in 1681, it is one of the oldest boroughs in Pennsylvania. Nearby [Croydon](/dentist-croydon-pa/) and [Levittown](/dentist-levittown-pa/) are about 13 and 16 minutes from the office.

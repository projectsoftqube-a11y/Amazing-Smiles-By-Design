## About Penndel

Penndel is a Bucks County borough, known as Attleboro and later South Langhorne before taking its current name in 1947. US-1 Business (Lincoln Highway), PA-413 and PA-513 all pass through the borough, and it shares ZIP 19047 with [Langhorne](/dentist-langhorne-pa/) and [Hulmeville](/dentist-hulmeville-pa/).

## Why Replace a Missing Tooth?

Missing teeth can change the way you chew and speak, and the gap lets neighboring teeth shift. Over time, the jawbone where a tooth is missing can also weaken. Replacing the tooth helps protect your bite and long-term oral health.

## Implant, Bridge or Denture?

| | Dental implant | Dental bridge | Denture |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it replaces | The root and the visible tooth | The visible tooth | Visible teeth and gum tissue |
| Supported by | A titanium or zirconia post in the jawbone | Crowns on neighboring teeth (or bonded wings, for a Maryland bridge) | The gums, clasps (partials) or implants |
| Neighboring teeth | Not changed | Usually prepared for crowns | Partials use clasps on nearby teeth |
| Jawbone | Helps preserve it by stimulating the bone | Doesn't stimulate the bone | Doesn't stimulate the bone (unless implant-supported) |
| Removable? | No | No | Yes, except screw-retained implant dentures |
| Treatment time | Several months for the implant to bond with the bone | Usually two to three visits | Varies; immediate dentures can be placed right after extractions |

The best choice depends on your gums, bone, neighboring teeth and budget. Learn more: [Implants](/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants/) · [Bridges](/restorative-dentistry/dental-bridges/) · [Dentures](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/)

## How Implant Treatment Works

Treatment is planned with detailed imaging, including CBCT scans. The implant is placed under local anesthesia (ask us about dental sedation options) and bonds with the bone over several months. An abutment and a custom crown, bridge or full-arch restoration complete it. With proper care, many implants remain functional for decades.

## Langhorne Implant FAQs

### How far is the implant office from Langhorne?
Amazing Smiles By Design is about 4.6 miles from Langhorne, typically a 10-minute drive, according to Google Maps.

### Is an implant better than a bridge?
Both replace missing teeth. An implant replaces the tooth root and doesn't change the neighboring teeth, while a bridge is anchored to the teeth beside the gap. The dentist will recommend the best option after examining your teeth, gums and jawbone.

### Do I need to replace a missing tooth right away?
There's no fixed deadline, but neighboring teeth can shift into the gap and the jawbone can weaken over time, so it's worth discussing your options soon.

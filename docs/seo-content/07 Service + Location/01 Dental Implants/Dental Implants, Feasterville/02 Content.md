## What Happens at Your Implant Consultation

Your first implant visit is about deciding whether implants are right for you and what the plan would be.

1. **Your goals and history.** We talk about which teeth are missing, what you'd like to achieve, and your medical history.
2. **An exam.** The dentist checks your gums, remaining teeth and bite. Healthy gums and good oral health matter for implant success.
3. **Detailed imaging.** Digital X-rays and, when needed, a Cone Beam CT (CBCT) scan show bone density and volume, nerves, sinus structures and the alignment of neighboring teeth.
4. **Your options.** Depending on what's found, that could be a single implant, an implant-supported bridge, a full-arch restoration, implant-supported dentures, or another option such as a [bridge](/restorative-dentistry/dental-bridges/) or [denture](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/).
5. **Your plan and cost.** You'll receive a personalized treatment plan, including whether bone grafting is needed first.

## Are You a Good Candidate?

Ideal implant candidates typically have one or more missing teeth, healthy gums, enough jawbone to support an implant, good overall oral health, and don't smoke or are willing to stop during healing. Older patients can still be candidates; what matters is bone and oral health, and the jaw must have finished growing.

## After the Consultation

If you go ahead, the implant is placed in a minor surgical procedure under local anesthesia (ask us about dental sedation options), heals and bonds with the bone over several months, and is then topped with your custom crown or restoration.

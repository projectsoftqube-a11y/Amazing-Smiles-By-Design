## Implant Options for Bucks County Patients

| Your situation | Implant option | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| One missing tooth | Single-tooth implant | A post replaces the root and supports a custom crown, without changing the teeth beside it |
| Several missing teeth | Implant-supported bridge | Implants anchor a bridge instead of relying on natural teeth |
| Most or all teeth missing | Full-arch restoration | A full row of teeth supported by a few strategically placed implants, including All-on-4 style restorations |
| Loose or uncomfortable dentures | [Implant-supported dentures](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/) | Ball, bar or screw-retained designs that can significantly reduce denture movement |

The right option depends on your teeth, gums, bone and goals. If bone density is too low, bone grafting may be recommended first. [How dental implants work](/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants/)

## Planned With In-Office 3D Imaging

Amazing Smiles By Design has Cone Beam CT (CBCT) imaging, digital X-rays and the RayFace facial scanner in the office. Before implant treatment, detailed imaging is used to assess:

- Bone density and volume
- The position of nearby nerves and sinus structures
- The alignment of the surrounding teeth
- The best angle and depth for each implant

## The Implant Timeline

1. **Consultation:** exam, medical history and imaging to decide whether implants are right for you.
2. **Implant placement:** a minor surgical procedure under local anesthesia. Ask us about dental sedation options.
3. **Healing:** over several months, the implant bonds with the bone (osseointegration). Most patients return to normal daily activities shortly after surgery.
4. **Final restoration:** an abutment and your custom crown, bridge or full-arch restoration are attached.

## Bucks County Implant FAQs

### Where can I get dental implants in Bucks County?
Amazing Smiles By Design provides dental implants at 3101 Bristol Road, Suite 1, Bensalem, in Lower Bucks County. Dr. Keyur Dudhat focuses on implant and cosmetic dentistry, and implant planning uses detailed imaging, with a CBCT scan when needed.

### Do you offer full-arch implants and implant-supported dentures?
Yes. For patients missing most or all of their teeth, full-arch restorations and implant-supported dentures can be supported by a few strategically placed implants.

### How long does the dental implant process take?
There are four stages: consultation and imaging, implant placement, several months of healing while the implant fuses with the bone, and attaching the final restoration.

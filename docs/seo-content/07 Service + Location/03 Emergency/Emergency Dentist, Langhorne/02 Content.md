## Toothache or Swelling: What to Do Now

Throbbing pain or swelling often means an infection, which can spread if it's left alone. While you contact us:

- **Rinse** your mouth with warm water.
- **Floss gently** to remove any food caught near the sore tooth.
- **Use a cold compress** on the outside of your cheek if there's swelling.
- **Don't put aspirin** or other painkillers directly on the gums or tooth.
- **Call or text (215) 639-5331.** Don't wait for the pain to pass.

## Is It an Emergency?

Pain that won't let you sleep, a swollen face or gum, a bad taste with a bump on the gum, or an injury to your teeth are all reasons to get seen urgently. Not sure? Text or call. ## What Happens at Your Emergency Visit

1. **Exam and digital X-rays** to find the cause.
2. **Pain relief** and, often, treatment started in the same visit whenever possible.
3. **A plan to fix it:** for an infection, that may be a [root canal](/restorative-dentistry/non-surgical-root-canal/) to save the tooth, or an [extraction](/general-dentistry/tooth-extraction/) if it can't be saved.

New patients can use the $59 Emergency Visit Special, which includes the necessary exam and X-rays.

## Until You Can Be Seen

These general first-aid steps can help while you contact us:

- **Knocked-out tooth:** pick it up by the crown (the part you chew with), not the root. If it's dirty, rinse it gently with water. Keep it moist, ideally in milk, and get care right away; within about an hour gives the best chance of saving it.
- **Broken or chipped tooth:** rinse your mouth with warm water, save any pieces, and use a cold compress on your cheek if there's swelling.
- **Bleeding:** apply gentle pressure with clean gauze. The mouth has many blood vessels, so small injuries can bleed a lot.
- **Lost filling or crown:** keep the crown if you have it, and avoid chewing on that side.

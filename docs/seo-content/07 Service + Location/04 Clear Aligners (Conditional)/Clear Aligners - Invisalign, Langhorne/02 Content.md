## Your Invisalign Timeline

| Stage | What happens | When |
|---|---|---|
| Consultation | Exam and a discussion of your goals to see if Invisalign is right for you | First visit |
| Scans and planning | Digital scans build a 3D model and a plan for how your teeth will move | If Invisalign is recommended |
| Fitting | Your custom aligners are checked; small tooth-colored attachments may be placed | When aligners are ready |
| Wearing aligners | 20 to 22 hours a day; switch to a new aligner about every one to two weeks | Throughout treatment |
| Check-ups | Progress checks and your next aligners | About every six weeks |
| Finish | Retainers, often clear, help keep teeth from shifting back | After about 9 to 15 months for many patients |

## What Invisalign Can Fix

Crooked or misaligned teeth, crowding, gaps, minor bite problems and teeth that have shifted over time. [Invisalign clear aligners](/cosmetic-dentistry/clear-aligners/)

## Daily Life With Aligners

- Wear them nearly all day; they come out for meals, drinks other than water, and cleaning your teeth.
- Eat what you like, because aligners come out for meals.
- Brush and floss normally; no special tools are needed.
- New trays can feel snug for the first few days as teeth move.

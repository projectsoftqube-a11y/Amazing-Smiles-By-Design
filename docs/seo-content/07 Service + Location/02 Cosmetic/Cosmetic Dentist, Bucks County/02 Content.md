## How a Cosmetic Consultation Works

1. **Tell us what you'd like to change.** Color, chips, gaps, uneven teeth, crowding or worn edges.
2. **Exam and records.** The dentist checks your oral health, bite and facial structure. For veneers, digital imaging and smile design technology can help you visualize how your final smile may appear.
3. **Your options.** You'll hear which treatments fit your goals, alone or combined in a smile makeover.
4. **Your plan.** A personalized treatment plan with timing and cost, plus financing options.

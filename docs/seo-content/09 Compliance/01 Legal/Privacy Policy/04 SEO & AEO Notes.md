## 4. Items the practice must supply

- date the policy is published
- if forms are delivered through a third-party service or practice-management system, name it here.
- how the practice uses phone numbers from calls and texts to (215) 639-5331; if it sends appointment reminders or marketing texts, describe consent and how to opt out, e.g. reply STOP.
- IP addresses and other such information are not linked to any personally identifiable information. Confirm this is still true with the analytics and advertising tools installed on the new site.
- list the tools the new site uses, for example Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads conversion tracking, Meta Pixel, Microsoft Clarity, call tracking.
- whether the practice sells or shares website information, and which service providers process it on the practice's behalf.

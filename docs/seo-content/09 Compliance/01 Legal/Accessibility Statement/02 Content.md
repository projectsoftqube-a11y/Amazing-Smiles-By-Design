# Accessibility Statement

We strive to make the Amazing Smiles By Design website universally accessible, and we are continuously working to improve the accessibility of our content. If any part of this website doesn't meet your needs, please call us at (215) 639-5331 and we'll help.

---

## Our Goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. [CONFIRM: the practice approves this target and the site was checked before launch.] Our goals include:

- Text alternatives for meaningful images
- Pages that can be used with a keyboard alone
- Sufficient color contrast and resizable text
- Clear headings, labels and link text
- Forms with labels and helpful error messages
- Captions or transcripts for video content, where provided

Some content, such as third-party tools and documents, may not yet fully meet these guidelines. [CONFIRM: how often the practice will review the site for accessibility.]

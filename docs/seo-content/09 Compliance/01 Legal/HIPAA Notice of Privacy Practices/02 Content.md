## Your Rights Over Your Health Information

Under HIPAA, you generally have the right to:

- **Get a copy** of your health records
- **Ask us to correct** health information you think is wrong or incomplete
- **Request confidential communications**, for example at a different phone number or address
- **Ask us to limit** what we use or share
- **Get a list** of those with whom we've shared your information
- **Get a copy** of this privacy notice
- **Choose someone** to act for you
- **File a complaint** if you believe your privacy rights have been violated

Our full notice explains each of these rights and how to use them.

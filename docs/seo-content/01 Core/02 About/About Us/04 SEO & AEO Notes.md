## 3. Local and AEO/GEO role of this page

- **Entity reinforcement.** The About page repeats the same core facts as the homepage: name, address, the dentist, his credentials, the services focus and hours. AI tools and Google cross-check facts across pages; consistent repetition is what makes them confident enough to state the facts.
- **E-E-A-T for a health site.** The page shows who provides care (a DMD from Temple University), his ongoing training, community service (Missions of Mercy) and the technology used. For YMYL (health) sites, Google's quality guidelines put weight on who is behind the content.
- **Shared schema graph.** The AboutPage schema reuses the homepage `@id`s for the business, website and dentist, so the pages describe one entity rather than several.
- **Local signals.** Identical NAP, hours and the same 7-town service list as the homepage and current site.

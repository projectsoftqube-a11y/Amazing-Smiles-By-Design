## 2. Local and AEO/GEO role

- **This is the page Google and AI tools use to check the business's core facts.** It carries the most complete, consistent NAP + hours + map data on the site, matching the homepage and (it must) the Google Business Profile.
- **ContactPage schema** with the full Dentist entity: address, phone, fax, opening hours, map link and an appointments contact point.
- **Direct-answer FAQs** for the questions people ask voice assistants and AI tools: address, phone, hours, "open Fridays/weekends?", how to book, emergencies.
- **"Closed Friday to Sunday" is stated explicitly** so AI tools don't guess weekend hours.

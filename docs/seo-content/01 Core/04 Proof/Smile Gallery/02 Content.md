## Treatments Behind a Smile Makeover

A smile makeover can combine one or more treatments. Cosmetic and restorative treatments we offer at our Bensalem office include:

- **[Teeth whitening](/cosmetic-dentistry/teeth-whitening/):** having a beautiful smile may be easier than you think. Many people achieve the look they've been dreaming of with our simple whitening procedure.
- **[Porcelain veneers](/cosmetic-dentistry/porcelain-veneers/):** thin shells of ceramic that bond directly to the front surfaces of the teeth, an ideal choice for improving your smile.
- **[Dental bonding](/cosmetic-dentistry/dental-bonding/):** tooth-colored material used to repair chips and reshape teeth.
- **[Clear aligners](/cosmetic-dentistry/clear-aligners/):** removable clear trays that gradually straighten teeth.
- **[Dental crowns](/restorative-dentistry/dental-crowns/):** a cap that covers and restores a damaged tooth. Our goal is to provide dentistry that is undetectable.
- **[Dental implants](/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants/):** a foundation for replacement teeth that look, feel and function like natural teeth.

## Smile Makeover FAQs

### What is a smile makeover?
A smile makeover is a plan to improve the appearance of your smile using one or more dental treatments, such as teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, dental bonding, clear aligners, crowns or implants.

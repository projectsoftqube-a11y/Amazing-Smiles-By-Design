## 2. How the page supports local search and AEO/GEO

- **Reputation queries.** People search "[practice] reviews" just before booking. Owning that result on your own site, with real words from real patients, keeps the decision on your domain.
- **"What Patients Mention Most" summary.** A short, checkable summary counted from the reviews on the page (friendly staff in 4 of 6, comfort in 3, hygienists in 2). AI tools answering "What do patients say about Amazing Smiles By Design?" can quote it directly. The counts are exact, not rounded up.
- **Entity tie-in.** The hero names the practice, Dr. Dudhat and the full address, so reviews attach to the right business.
- **Honest review handling.** No star ratings, review counts or review schema that can't be verified. Google's review-snippet policy excludes self-serving reviews, and AI tools cross-check ratings against Google and Yelp. A mismatch hurts trust.
- **The bigger local lever is off-site:** the volume, recency and rating of reviews on the Google Business Profile drive local-pack rankings far more than this page does.

## 2. How the page supports local search and AEO/GEO

- **Person entity, done properly.** Name, degree, school, undergraduate school, birthplace, clinical focus and employer appear as visible text and in the Person schema. The `@id` is shared with the homepage and About page, so Google and AI tools see one person connected to one business.
- **"At a Glance" fact table.** Short label/value facts are the easiest format for answer engines to extract. Questions like "Where did Dr. Dudhat go to dental school?" get a direct answer.
- **FAQs written as people ask them** about a dentist: where he practices, his school, his focus, and how to book.
- **E-E-A-T for a health site:** credentials, ongoing training, community service, and a link to the practice's technology. All of it comes from the current site, with nothing inflated.
- **Local tie-in:** the full address and phone in the hero and FAQ, "in Bensalem" in the CTA, and the Dentist node in the schema.
- **No specialist language.** "Special focus" is the site's own wording. The page never says "specialist", "specializes" or "board-certified"; in dentistry those terms imply a recognised specialty the site doesn't claim.

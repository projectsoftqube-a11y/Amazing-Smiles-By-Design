## Education and Background

Keyur Dudhat was born and raised in Lansdale, Pennsylvania. He completed his undergraduate studies at Penn State University before earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Temple University. Today he cares for patients at Amazing Smiles By Design in Bensalem.

---

## Clinical Focus: Implant and Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Dudhat is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. He provides comprehensive dental care with a special focus on two areas:

- **Dental implants:** a foundation for replacement teeth that look, feel and function like natural teeth. [Learn about dental implants](/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants/)
- **Cosmetic dentistry:** improving the look of your smile with treatments such as [porcelain veneers](/cosmetic-dentistry/porcelain-veneers/), [teeth whitening](/cosmetic-dentistry/teeth-whitening/) and [dental bonding](/cosmetic-dentistry/dental-bonding/). [All cosmetic dentistry](/cosmetic-dentistry/)

He also provides everyday [general and family dentistry](/general-dentistry/) and [restorative care](/restorative-dentistry/) such as crowns, fillings and root canals.

## Committed to Ongoing Training

Dedicated to staying at the forefront of modern dentistry, Dr. Dudhat regularly pursues advanced training in procedures such as dental implants and veneers.

His care is supported by the diagnostic technology in our office: cone beam CT (CBCT) scans, digital X-rays and the RayFace facial scanner, which let the dental team view teeth, bone, nerves and facial structures in remarkable detail.

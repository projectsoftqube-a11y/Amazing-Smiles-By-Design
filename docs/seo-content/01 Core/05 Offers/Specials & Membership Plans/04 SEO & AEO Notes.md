## 2. Local and AEO/GEO role

- **Price questions are among the most common things people ask AI tools about a dentist** ("How much is a cleaning without insurance near me?"). This page answers with exact, checkable facts: three plan prices, what each includes, the 20% member discount and its exclusions, and the $59 new-patient emergency special.
- **The comparison table** gives search engines and AI tools structured facts to quote. It's built as a real HTML table, never an image.
- **Offer schema** lists the four plans with prices, matching the page and the homepage schema, so the same prices appear everywhere.
- **Local:** "in Bensalem" in the title and H1, full NAP in the final CTA, and the Dentist entity in the schema.

## 3. New fact found on the current site

The Specials page says members get **20% off "all other dental procedures, excluding dental implants and Invisalign treatment."** The practice names Invisalign, so it very likely offers it. Worth confirming before writing the Clear Aligners pages, because it decides whether those pages can use the "Invisalign" keywords (110,000/mo nationally for "invisalign near me").

## Repairing Damaged Teeth

When a tooth is decayed, cracked or worn, the right repair depends on how much healthy tooth is left:

- [Dental fillings](/restorative-dentistry/dental-fillings/): tooth-colored composite fillings for cavities and small chips or cracks.
- [Inlays and onlays](/restorative-dentistry/inlays-onlays/): custom restorations for teeth with too much damage for a filling but not enough to need a full crown.
- [Dental crowns](/restorative-dentistry/dental-crowns/): a custom cap that covers and protects a weak, cracked or heavily filled tooth.

---

## Saving an Infected Tooth

When the soft tissue inside a tooth becomes infected or inflamed, [non-surgical root canal therapy](/restorative-dentistry/non-surgical-root-canal/) removes the infection so you can keep your natural tooth instead of having it extracted. Many teeth then need a crown for strength.

---

## Replacing Missing Teeth

- [Dental implants](/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants/): a titanium or zirconia post that replaces the root of a missing tooth and supports a custom crown, bridge or full-arch restoration.
- [Dental bridges](/restorative-dentistry/dental-bridges/): a fixed replacement tooth anchored to the healthy teeth on either side of the gap.
- [Dentures](/restorative-dentistry/dentures/): full, partial, immediate and implant-supported dentures.

## Which Restorative Treatment Is Right for You?

| Your situation | Options to discuss |
|---|---|
| A small cavity, chip or crack | Filling |
| Moderate damage to the chewing surface | Inlay or onlay |
| A weak, cracked or root-canal-treated tooth | Crown |
| Severe tooth pain or an infected tooth | Root canal, then usually a crown |
| One missing tooth | Implant or bridge |
| Several or all teeth missing | Partial, full or implant-supported dentures; full-arch implants |

The dentist will examine your teeth, take digital X-rays or a CBCT scan when needed, and explain the options that fit your mouth, goals and budget.

## Restorative Dentistry FAQs

### What is restorative dentistry?
Restorative dentistry is the branch of dentistry that repairs damaged teeth and replaces missing teeth. It includes fillings, inlays and onlays, crowns, root canals, bridges, dental implants and dentures.

### What are my options for replacing a missing tooth?
The main options are a dental implant, a dental bridge or a partial denture. The dentist will recommend the best option after examining your teeth, gums and jawbone.

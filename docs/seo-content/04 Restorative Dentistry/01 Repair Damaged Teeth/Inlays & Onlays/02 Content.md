# Inlays & Onlays in Bensalem

Inlays and onlays are custom restorations made in a dental laboratory to repair damage on the chewing surface of a tooth. They are often a conservative alternative to a crown when the tooth needs more support than a filling can give. At Amazing Smiles By Design in Bensalem, PA, inlays and onlays are used to restore teeth with moderate damage while preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible.

## Inlay vs. Onlay vs. Crown

| | Inlay | Onlay | Crown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Covers | The grooves in the center of the chewing surface, inside the cusp tips | The chewing surface plus one or more cusps (the raised chewing edges) | The whole visible tooth |
| Used when | Damage is in the center of the tooth | Damage extends to the cusps | The tooth needs full coverage and protection |
| Natural tooth preserved | Most | More than a crown | Least |

Inlays work like fillings but are custom-made for a more precise fit and a stronger restoration. Compare: [Fillings](/restorative-dentistry/dental-fillings/) · [Crowns](/restorative-dentistry/dental-crowns/)

## Materials

Inlays and onlays can be made from porcelain, composite resin or gold. Porcelain is one of the most common choices because it combines strength and durability with a natural, tooth-colored look that can be closely matched to your teeth.

---

## When Inlays or Onlays Are Recommended

They are typically recommended when damage from decay, a fracture or an older filling is too large for a regular filling, for example:

- Large cavities that can't be restored with a regular filling
- Broken or cracked teeth
- Old fillings that need to be replaced
- Damage to the chewing surface
- Teeth that need reinforcement but don't need a crown

---

## The Inlay and Onlay Process

Placing an inlay or onlay usually takes two visits.

### First appointment
The damaged or decayed part of the tooth, and any old filling, is removed. An impression or digital scan is taken and sent to a dental laboratory, and a temporary restoration protects the tooth.

### Second appointment
The temporary is removed, and the inlay or onlay is checked for fit and bite. It is then permanently bonded with a high-strength dental resin and polished to a smooth, natural finish.

## Inlay and Onlay FAQs

### What is the difference between an inlay and an onlay?
An inlay fits within the grooves of the chewing surface, inside the cusp tips. An onlay covers a larger area, including one or more cusps, while still preserving more natural tooth than a full crown.

### Is an onlay better than a crown?
When the remaining tooth structure is strong enough, an onlay can restore the tooth while preserving more natural tooth than a crown. If the tooth needs full coverage, the dentist may recommend a crown instead.

### How long do inlays and onlays last?
Inlays and onlays can last 10 to 30 years with proper dental care.

### How many visits does an inlay or onlay take?
Usually two: one to prepare the tooth and take an impression or digital scan, and one to bond the custom restoration.

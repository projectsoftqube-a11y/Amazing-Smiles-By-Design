## 3. Local and AEO/GEO signals

- The first sentence defines the service in a self-contained way (easy for AI tools to quote).
- "Bensalem, PA" plus the full address and phone are in visible text and match the Dentist schema.
- FAQ answers are short, direct and name the practice where the answer is practice-specific.
- No prices are quoted for restorative procedures (none on the current site); the 20% member discount is stated with its exclusion of implants and Invisalign.

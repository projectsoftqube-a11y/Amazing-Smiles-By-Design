# Dental Bridges in Bensalem

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth with an artificial tooth, called a pontic, that is held in place by the healthy teeth next to the gap, called abutment teeth. At Amazing Smiles By Design in Bensalem, PA, dental bridges are custom designed to match the shape, size and color of your natural teeth, using durable materials such as porcelain, ceramic or porcelain fused to metal.

## Why Replacing a Missing Tooth Matters

Teeth work together to keep proper spacing and bite alignment. When a tooth is lost, the teeth around it can shift toward the empty space, which can lead to:

- Bite misalignment
- A higher risk of gum disease
- Difficulty chewing certain foods
- Speech challenges
- Extra wear on the remaining teeth

---

## Benefits of a Dental Bridge

- Restores the appearance of your smile
- Improves chewing and speaking
- Keeps neighboring teeth from shifting
- Supports facial structure and helps prevent a sunken look
- A durable, long-lasting tooth replacement

---

## Types of Dental Bridges

- **Traditional bridge:** the most common type. Crowns are placed on the natural teeth next to the gap, and the replacement tooth is anchored between them.
- **Cantilever bridge:** supported by a crown on only one neighboring tooth; used when only one natural tooth is next to the space.
- **Maryland bonded bridge:** a conservative option that uses metal or porcelain wings bonded to the back of nearby teeth instead of crowns.

At your consultation, the dental team will evaluate your oral health and recommend the best type of bridge for your smile.

---

## The Dental Bridge Process

A dental bridge usually takes two to three visits.

1. **Preparation:** the teeth next to the gap are prepared to support the bridge, and impressions are taken so the bridge matches your bite.
2. **Temporary bridge:** a temporary bridge may be placed while your permanent bridge is made in a dental laboratory.
3. **Final bridge:** the permanent bridge is fitted and bonded into place.

---

## Bridge vs. Dental Implant

A bridge relies on the neighboring teeth for support, so those teeth are prepared for crowns (except with a Maryland bridge). A [dental implant](/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants/) replaces the tooth root with a post in the jawbone and doesn't change the teeth beside it. The dentist will help you compare both options for your situation.

---

## Caring for Your Bridge

Because a bridge depends on the supporting teeth, keeping them healthy is essential:

- Brush twice a day
- Floss around the bridge and the supporting teeth
- Keep up regular dental cleanings and exams

With good care, a dental bridge can remain a reliable tooth replacement for many years.

## Dental Bridge FAQs

### What is a dental bridge?
A dental bridge is a fixed restoration that replaces one or more missing teeth with an artificial tooth (pontic) anchored to the healthy teeth next to the gap.

### How long do dental bridges last?
With good oral care and routine dental visits, a dental bridge can remain a reliable tooth replacement for many years.

### How many visits does a bridge take?
A dental bridge usually takes two to three visits to prepare the supporting teeth, place a temporary bridge if needed and bond the final bridge.

### Is a bridge or an implant better?
Both replace missing teeth. A bridge is anchored to neighboring teeth, while an implant replaces the root and doesn't affect the teeth beside it. The dentist will recommend the best option after examining your teeth, gums and jawbone.

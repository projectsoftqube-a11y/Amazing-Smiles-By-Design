## Why Replace Missing Teeth?

Missing several teeth or a whole arch can make eating difficult, change your speech and affect your confidence. Missing teeth can also cause:

- Difficulty chewing and speaking
- Shifting of the remaining teeth
- Changes in bite alignment
- Extra stress on the remaining teeth
- Jaw muscle discomfort or headaches
- Changes in facial structure

---

## Full Dentures

Full dentures replace an entire arch of teeth. Artificial teeth are attached to a gum-colored acrylic base. Upper dentures cover the roof of the mouth (palate) to create suction and stability. Lower dentures are horseshoe-shaped to leave room for the tongue. Some patients need time to adjust, but modern designs fit far more comfortably than older versions.

---

## Partial Dentures

A partial denture replaces one or several missing teeth when some natural teeth remain. It is usually held in place with clasps on nearby teeth and helps keep the remaining teeth from shifting into the gaps. Partials can be made from metal and acrylic, which is thinner and stronger, or from acrylic alone, which is sometimes used as a temporary option.

---

## Immediate Dentures

When your remaining teeth need to be extracted, an immediate denture lets you avoid going without teeth while you heal. Impressions are taken before the extractions, and the denture is made in advance and placed right after your teeth are removed. Because the gums and bone change as they heal, adjustments or relines are often needed during healing.

---

## Implant-Supported Dentures

For more stability, dentures can attach to [dental implants](/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants/) placed in the jawbone. This greatly reduces movement when you chew and speak.

- **Ball attachment dentures:** two implants; the denture snaps on and can still be removed for cleaning.
- **Bar attachment dentures:** several implants connected by a bar; the denture clips on for greater stability.
- **Screw-retained dentures:** in some cases, five or more implants support a fixed denture that only a dentist removes during maintenance visits.

Implant-supported upper dentures may not need to cover the palate, which can make eating more comfortable and help you taste food better.

---

## Dentures vs. Implants

Traditional dentures rest on the gums and are removable. Implant-supported dentures and full-arch implants attach to posts in the jawbone for a more secure fit. The dentist will help you choose based on your oral health, bone and goals.

## Caring for Your Dentures

- Brush your dentures with a soft denture brush
- Use a denture cleaning cream instead of abrasive toothpaste
- Rinse with cool or lukewarm water. Avoid hot water, which can warp dentures
- Keep dentures moist when you aren't wearing them
- Attend routine dental exams

---

## Denture Relines, Liners and Exams

Over time, natural changes in the jawbone and gums can make dentures loose.

- **Hard relines:** the inside of the denture is reshaped to fit the contours of your gums.
- **Soft relines:** a flexible material cushions tender gums and sore spots.
- **Temporary relines:** a medicated reline lets swollen or irritated tissue heal before a permanent adjustment.
- **Soft denture liners:** a flexible cushion between the denture and gums for sensitive gums, flattened ridges or bony areas.

Relines are typically recommended every one to two years. An annual denture exam checks your gums, bone, the fit and bite of your denture, wear or damage, and any signs of oral abnormalities.

## Denture FAQs

### What types of dentures do you offer?
Amazing Smiles By Design in Bensalem, PA offers full dentures, partial dentures, immediate dentures and implant-supported dentures, along with denture relines, soft liners and annual denture exams.

### Can I get dentures the same day my teeth are removed?
Immediate dentures are made in advance from impressions taken before your extractions and are placed right after your teeth are removed, so you don't have to go without teeth while you heal.

### How often do dentures need to be relined?
Relines are typically recommended every one to two years to maintain the best fit.

### What are implant-supported dentures?
Implant-supported dentures attach to dental implants placed in the jawbone. They move much less than traditional dentures when you chew and speak.

### How do I clean my dentures?
Brush them with a soft denture brush and a denture cleaning cream, rinse with cool or lukewarm water, and keep them moist when you aren't wearing them. Avoid hot water, which can warp dentures.
